August 12, 2026 · All work committed & pushed

1. What was accomplished

Completed the volunteer attendance feature end-to-end, per the plan: volunteers check themselves in at the venue via a printed QR, attendance is tracked per day and per event, and admins manage it all from a dedicated screen. Also delivered shareable API documentation for the mobile team.

  • Venue QR check-in — public page (/check-in/:eventId, no login): scan QR → enter phone → confirm identity (name + photo + seva) → pick the day → confirm attendance.
  • Per-day attendance — a multi-day festival records one entry per day the volunteer attends (dayAttendance on the registration).
  • Admin attendance page (/admin/attendance) — event + day selectors, per-day stats, check-in / no-show / undo actions, assign seva on the spot, and a check-in QR button.
  • Rule updates — any registered volunteer can check in; a no-show volunteer who scans the QR is recorded as attended; mistaken check-ins/no-shows can be undone; service coordinators can mark attendance.
  • Mobile team docs — dedicated API docs (Markdown, HTML, PDF) for Register, My Seva, time slots, and check-in.

2. The volunteer check-in flow

Venue QR → /check-in/<EVENT_CODE>
   └─ Volunteer enters registered phone number
        └─ "Is this you?" — name, photo, assigned seva
             └─ Pick the day (chips, defaults to today)
                  └─ Confirm Attendance → day recorded

Any volunteer with a registration can check in — even without an assigned service. The admin sees who is present and assigns sevas on the spot.
